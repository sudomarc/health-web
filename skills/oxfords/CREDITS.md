# Credits — Oxfords design system

This prototype incorporates the **Oxfords** UI design system (MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 Arham Hafeez)
as its interface build/review reference. The Oxfords files live in `skills/oxfords/` and
`adapters/`. This project's own design system, tokens and brand rules take priority over
Oxfords; Oxfords is used only for what the clinic's identity leaves open.

Oxfords sources:
- https://github.com/arhamhi/oxfords (skills/oxfords/, adapters/)
- CREDITS.md within the Oxfords repository (Kole Jain, impeccable, taste-skill, apple-design)

The clinic prototype itself (International Clinic Conakry, `index.html`, `styles.css`,
`src/`) remains an independent, unofficial prototype: no medical data, no backend, no
storage, and the anti-indexation / prototype notice stay in place until publication is
authorised.
